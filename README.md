# 🎓 ScholarCast — Predictive Learning Intelligence for Educators

> _Forecasting tomorrow's classrooms today — one learner at a time._

## 🌟 Overview

**ScholarCast** is a forward-looking academic intelligence platform that takes the same spark of inspiration behind GradeGuru and reimagines it as a full classroom forecasting ecosystem. Where simple predictors stop at a single number, ScholarCast paints a portrait — blending attendance rhythms, assignment cadence, engagement signals, and historical performance into a composite forecast that educators can actually act on.

Think of it as a weather station for learning outcomes. Instead of telling you it will rain tomorrow, it tells you _which students need an umbrella, which ones are ready to sprint in the sunshine, and which classroom conditions will bring the storm._ 🌦️📚

Scholarship is not a lottery — it is a pattern. ScholarCast helps teachers read the pattern before the semester writes its ending.

---

## 💡 Why ScholarCast Exists

Traditional grade books are rearview mirrors. They tell you what already happened. By the time the final exam lands, intervention windows have usually closed.

ScholarCast flips the mirror. It uses supervised regression and ensemble learning to produce **forward-looking performance bands** for each learner, along with confidence intervals and recommended nudges. The goal is not surveillance — it is **scaffolding**.

Educators deserve tools that respect their time. ScholarCast returns insight in under a second, with no arcane configuration required.

## 🗺️ How It Thinks

ScholarCast approaches the problem in four movements:

1. **Ingestion** — raw records flow from CSV, JSON, or LMS exports into a normalized schema.
2. **Featurization** — attendance ratio, assignment completion cadence, quiz variance, study-hour estimates, prior GPA, and engagement proxies are synthesized.
3. **Inference** — an ensemble of estimators produces a weighted central forecast plus a dispersion band.
4. **Narration** — the insights layer translates numbers into human-readable guidance.

Each stage is inspectable. Nothing is a black box that shrugs when asked "why."

## 🛠️ Getting Started (Conceptual Walkthrough)

Setting up ScholarCast is intentionally gentle. Rather than a wall of terminal incantations, the flow is:

- Bring your learner dataset into the workspace folder.
- Open the desktop shell or launch the browser-based studio.
- Point the importer at your file; ScholarCast auto-detects the schema.
- Map your columns to the internal fields using the guided wizard.
- Choose a forecast horizon — midterm, semester, or exam-specific.
- Review the ensemble output and export your first report.

No cryptic flags. No kitchen-sink configuration. Just a guided path from data to decision. 🚀

## 🌐 Responsive UI in Practice

The interface was designed around a simple principle: a teacher should be able to glance at a tablet while walking between desks and still read the signal. Layouts reflow, tables become cards, charts simplify at narrow widths, and color contrast remains accessible under bright classroom lighting.

## 🗣️ Multilingual Support

Strings live in plain translation files, so adding a new language is a matter of copying one template and editing text. Right-to-left layouts are honored automatically. Locale-aware number and date formatting is applied across the dashboard.

## 🔒 Privacy & Ethics

Predictions about people deserve care. ScholarCast ships with:

- Local-first processing
- Explicit opt-in for any sync
- No third-party telemetry by default
- Clear documentation on bias risks and mitigation strategies
- Practical guidance discouraging use of forecasts as the sole basis for high-stakes decisions

A forecast is a compass, not a verdict. 🧭
